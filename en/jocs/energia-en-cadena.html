⚡ Energia en Cadena

Potential, kinetic, mechanical, electrical: follow the energy inside each power station.

Technology Ages 13-14 🗣 Game in Catalan Free
Play now ▶

What it teaches

How is energy transformed inside a hydroelectric plant, a wind farm, a solar photovoltaic plant, a solar thermal plant, a fossil-fuel plant or a nuclear plant? This game has 110 questions about the six power stations: energy transformation chains, how each one works step by step, what each component does, and sustainability, environmental impact and the measures that reduce it.

There are eight question types: pick the right answer, true or false, which explanation is right, find the wrong word in a sentence, match, put the process in order, find the odd one out and complete the energy chain. Every answer comes with a short explanation and, often, a photo of a real power station.

It can be played in two ways. Projected for the whole class, with 1 to 6 teams: the teacher asks who says each option, assigns the teams and the game marks the answers itself. Or 1 to 6 students on a single computer, taking turns, with rebounds, a joker and points for speed. Designed for the energy sources unit in the second year of secondary school. The game interface is in Catalan.

There is also a Study Zone («Zona d'estudi») where each student can study alone, with no points or rankings. It has a theory sheet for each power station (energy chain, step by step, components, pros and cons, environmental impact), 130 flashcards that come back if you don't know them yet, a practice mode with the game's questions that marks each answer at once and repeats the ones you got wrong, and a challenge to build the energy chain of each power station.

Features

  • 110 questions on six power stations, plus a block comparing them
  • 8 question types, including energy chains and find-the-error
  • Projected mode: the teacher assigns teams and the game marks itself
  • Group mode: 1-6 players on one computer, taking turns, with rebounds and a joker
  • Study Zone for studying alone: theory, flashcards, practice and build-the-chain
  • An explanation for every question and photos of real power stations
  • Free, no sign-up, no ads; a single file that works offline
🔒 Progress is stored in the browser. The optional class sync is switched off: no data leaves the device. 📱 An Android APK is available on request. Made by Robert Potau, secondary school teacher of Technology. © 2026 Robert Potau Nuñez — All rights reserved.
