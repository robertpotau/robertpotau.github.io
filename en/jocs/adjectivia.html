🦉 Adjectivia

Happy, furious, thrilled: English adjectives and feelings, played on the whiteboard.

English Ages 12-14 🗣 Game in English Free
🚧 Under construction 🚧

What it teaches

Adjectivia is made to be played in class on the digital whiteboard, with one to six students taking turns in front of the class. There are nine game modes: the 15-question Golden Ladder with five lifelines (50:50, ask the class, phone a friend, swap the question and double chance), Memory, Match-Up, Emotion Faces, Sentence Gap, Speed Round, Hot Seat for speaking practice, Balloon Pop and a Word Bank with flashcards.

The vocabulary has 304 adjectives in 14 topics — feelings, personality, appearance, size, senses, colours, time… — each with an English definition, an example sentence, synonyms, opposites and three levels. Feelings are practised with illustrated faces, and a button shows the Catalan translation when it is needed.

Every player has their own name and avatar, experience points, seven ranks and sixteen trophies, and every game ends with a podium and a list of words to review. Free and sign-up-free, like the whole collection.

Features

  • 304 adjectives and feelings in 14 topics
  • 9 game modes designed for the whiteboard
  • 1 to 6 players with editable names and avatars
  • Five lifelines in the Golden Ladder
  • Emotion faces, pronunciation and Catalan help
  • Free, no sign-up, no ads
🔒 Progress is stored in the browser. The optional class sync is switched off: no data leaves the device. 📱 An Android APK is available on request. Made by Robert Potau, secondary school teacher of Technology. © 2026 Robert Potau Nuñez — All rights reserved.
