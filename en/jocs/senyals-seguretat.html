🔍 Detectiu de Senyals

Colours, shapes and pictograms: learn to read workshop and building-site safety signs.

Technology Ages 12-13 🗣 Game in Catalan Free
Play now ▶

What it teaches

Knowing what a safety sign means — its colour, its shape and what the pictogram says — is essential for moving safely around the technology workshop and the building site. This game includes 51 signs: prohibition, warning, mandatory, rescue or first aid and fire-fighting equipment, with the ISO 7010 pictograms.

There is a study area, with a catalogue of sign cards and flashcards that bring back the signs students find hardest, and a practice area with eight modes: colours and meaning, shape and features, building a sign piece by piece, black-and-white silhouettes, memory, workshop inspector, hazard → protective equipment (PPE) and a countdown challenge that mixes every question for as long as the student chooses.

Every question explains why the answer is right. Designed for the first year of secondary school and for individual play on a phone, tablet or computer. Each student has a profile with ranks, trophies and statistics, and progress is saved in the browser. The game interface is in Catalan.

Features

  • 51 ISO 7010 signs of five types, each with its own card
  • Catalogue and flashcards that repeat the hardest signs
  • 8 modes: Colours, Shape, Build, Silhouettes, Memory, Inspector and more
  • Hazard → PPE and Workshop Inspector, with step-by-step explanations
  • Countdown challenge: pick the time and answer as many questions as you can
  • Free, no sign-up, no ads, and works offline
🔒 Progress is stored in the browser. The optional class sync is switched off: no data leaves the device. 📱 An Android APK is available on request. Made by Robert Potau, secondary school teacher of Technology. © 2026 Robert Potau Nuñez — All rights reserved.
