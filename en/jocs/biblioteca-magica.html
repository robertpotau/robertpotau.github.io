📚 Biblioteca màgica

Practise reading and vocabulary through popular tales, in a magic 3D library.

Reading Ages 12-14 🗣 Game in Catalan Free
Play now ▶

What it teaches

Biblioteca màgica is a game for practising reading and vocabulary through popular tales. Each chapter of a public-domain classic tale — for now, Alice's adventures — can be read in Catalan at three levels (Cautious, Brave and Daring): the same story, with simpler sentences or richer vocabulary depending on the reader.

Difficult words, set phrases and puns in the text are highlighted: tapping them saves a collectible card with a definition, a synonym and an example. The cards go into an album inside the 3D library, and every chapter ends with a quiz and a word-matching challenge.

The text can be listened to in a synthetic Catalan voice, female or male. It runs in the browser with no sign-up and no ads, and progress is saved on the same device. The texts, adaptations, glossary, cards and design are original work by Robert Potau Nuñez with all rights reserved: the game is free to play, but may not be copied, redistributed or reused without permission (see the Terms of Use).

Features

  • Popular tales in Catalan, at three reading levels
  • Catalan voice to listen to every paragraph
  • 200+ vocabulary and set-phrase cards to collect
  • Comprehension and word-matching challenge in each chapter
  • 3D library with a card album
  • Free, no sign-up, no ads
🔒 Progress is stored in the browser. The optional class sync is switched off: no data leaves the device. 📱 An Android APK is available on request. Made by Robert Potau, secondary school teacher of Technology. © 2026 Robert Potau Nuñez — All rights reserved.
