🧰 Eines del taller

Photo, name and family: learn the workshop tools by studying and playing.

Technology Ages 12-13 🗣 Game in Catalan Free
Play now ▶

What it teaches

Knowing the workshop tools — which family they belong to, what they are called and what they are for — is the first step to working safely in Technology class. This game covers 39 tools in 10 usual families: striking, cutting, abrasion, drilling, gripping, screwing, passive tools, measuring, soldering and protection.

There is a study area, with a catalogue and flashcards that bring back the tools each student finds hardest, and a practice and competition area with eight games: sorting, memory, matching, a question ladder, a speed round and more. Each card shows a different photo of the same tool so students learn to recognise the tool, not one particular picture.

Designed for the first year of secondary school and for playing on the whiteboard with 1 to 6 players, or individually. Every group has profiles with experience points and trophies, and progress is saved in the browser. The game interface is in Catalan.

Features

  • 39 tools and 10 families from the technology workshop
  • Catalogue and flashcards that repeat the hardest tools
  • 8 games: Sort, Memory, Match, Tool Ladder and more
  • A different photo of the same tool on every card
  • 1 to 6 players on the whiteboard, profiles and trophies
  • Free, no sign-up, no ads, and works offline
🔒 Progress is stored in the browser. The optional class sync is switched off: no data leaves the device. 📱 An Android APK is available on request. Made by Robert Potau, secondary school teacher of Technology. © 2026 Robert Potau Nuñez — All rights reserved.
